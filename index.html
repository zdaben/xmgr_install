Systemd Service Diagnostics

Quick commands to inspect active socket listeners, unit status, and recent journal entries.

ss -tlnp
systemctl status --no-pager
journalctl -n 50 --no-pager

Kernel TCP & BBR Check

Verify active queue discipline and congestion control parameters on Debian/Ubuntu hosts.

sysctl net.core.default_qdisc
sysctl net.ipv4.tcp_congestion_control

TLS Handshake Inspection

Validate ALPN negotiation and TLS 1.3 certificate chain from the command line.

openssl s_client -connect 127.0.0.1:443 -alpn h2 </dev/null
